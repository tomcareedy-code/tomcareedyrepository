How It Works

Four steps to algorithmic growth

A dual approach of playlist promotion and direct track ads that compounds stream volume on your release.

1

Custom Playlist Creation

We create playlists curated around your genre and sound. Each playlist is designed to attract listeners who already love music like yours.

2

Targeted Ad Campaigns

We run paid ads to each playlist & recent releases, targeting audiences built around similar artists and relevant locations. Alongside playlist promotion, we run direct track ads targeting Tier 1 and Tier 2 markets, high-value listeners in regions that carry the most algorithmic weight. This dual approach compounds stream volume on your advertised track, increasing its reach and engagement simultaneously. Campaigns are optimised every 4 days for maximum efficiency.

3

Algorithm Activation

Quality streams from engaged listeners signal to Spotify that your music belongs alongside top artists in your genre. As stream volume and listener engagement grow across both your playlists and direct track promotion, Spotify's algorithm begins surfacing your music in Radio sessions, Autoplay, and editorial features like Discover Weekly.

4

Profile Growth

Your artist profile builds genuine, concentrated listenership, the kind that grows your monthly listeners, saves, and follows organically.

Why This Works

The Spotify algorithm, explained

Spotify's algorithm is driven by Radio streams. When listeners engage with your music through curated playlists and direct track promotion, Spotify begins placing you alongside similar artists in Autoplay and Radio sessions. This creates a compounding effect:

01

Listeners who enjoy similar artists discover your music naturally.

02

Direct track promotion to Tier 1 and Tier 2 markets accelerates stream volume, increasing the speed at which Spotify registers your track as relevant.

03

Consistent, engaged streams signal to Spotify that your music belongs in the algorithm.

04

Your artist profile builds a concentrated, engaged fanbase — not random one-off plays.

05

Algorithmic playlists like Discover Weekly and Radio start featuring you more frequently.
